# iOS Build Health & Developer Velocity

## Context
As codebase complexity grew, slower local builds and flaky CI reduced engineering throughput and confidence.

## Goal
Shorten iteration loops and improve CI signal quality for faster, safer product delivery.

## My Role
- Led cross-team efforts to identify build bottlenecks and flaky test categories.
- Balanced platform investment against product roadmap commitments.
- Shared best practices and guardrails with partner teams.

## What I Did
- Profiled build/test pipelines to identify expensive and unstable stages.
- Reduced avoidable incremental rebuilds with dependency boundary improvements.
- Improved CI observability and triage paths for recurring flaky patterns.
- Drove process changes for ownership and faster remediation of build health regressions.

## Outcome
- Improved median local build and test iteration times.
- Reduced flake-related CI failures.
- Increased engineering efficiency and confidence in shipping.

## Takeaways
Developer productivity investments compound rapidly when paired with clear ownership and measurable targets.
